## What each check actually established

**Terminal SOC equality** is checked directly, not inferred: after training, the
day is re-settled through `counterfactual_eval.settle_day` and each unit's
final SOC is compared with its initial one. The worst gap over both seeds is
below 1e-6.

**No corner collapse** is the check this run existed for. Mean normalized
deviation is 0.50–0.53 — the fleet is declaring quotes around the *middle* of
its box, not on the truthful corner — and only 0.1% of actions sit within 2% of
it. The retired logit-quadratic penalty could not have produced this: its
gradient grew with distance from the reference, so it pulled hardest exactly
where the policy was trying to explore.

**Valid SOC** is checked against each unit's own `soc_min` / `soc_max`, so a
clearing that respected the constraint only loosely would still be caught.

## One observation to carry into the full run

**The fleet's state of charge never rises above its initial 0.5 in either
seed** — the range is exactly `[0.100, 0.500]` both times, while `soc_max` is
0.90. Over these 60 episodes the fleet only ever discharges net.

This is not a failure of any check: the SOC stays inside its bounds and the
clearing is stable. It is also not structural — the B3 ladder's units charge
(0.81 MWh at bus 2, and so on) and their SOC does rise above 0.5, so nothing in
the mechanism prevents it.

The likely reading is that at this point in training the policies are still
declaring a high enough charge price to decline most charging, so the fleet
sells down its opening inventory. **The full run must show the SOC rising above
0.5 on some days, or the fleet is not arbitraging and the thing being measured
is not what the research is about.** Worth an explicit check in the first full
run rather than assuming the smoke run's 60 episodes were simply too few.

## Spread: narrower than the calibration predicted

The calibration's frozen-critic probe left a quote spread of 65.4 CNY/MWh at
`lambda = 0.25`; after 60 real episodes of gradient descent the spread is 21–23
CNY/MWh. The two are not the same quantity — one is where a fixed arbitrary
critic plus the regularizer settles, the other is where an improving critic
plus the regularizer settles — so the gap is expected rather than alarming.

It does mean the "non-degenerate" check's threshold (1 CNY/MWh) is a weak one.
At 21 CNY/MWh the fleet's quotes span about 14% of the discharge box and 7% of
the charge box: distinct per unit, but materially tighter than untrained. The
full run should report the spread per evaluation so a continuing collapse is
visible as a trend rather than only as a threshold crossing.
