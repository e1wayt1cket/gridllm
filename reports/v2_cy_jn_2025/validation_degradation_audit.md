## Findings

- The final mean is -77.7 but the median is -4.6; the mean is driven by a small number of extreme negative days.
- The three worst final days sum to -868.9, slightly more negative than the total final sum -854.9; the other eight days are close to flat or positive. The mean and median therefore diverge.
- The winter tail dominates: 2022-12-30, 2022-12-31 and 2022-12-13 worsen sharply. The summer/autumn days carry small or positive deltas.
- Best-round (R2) vs final (R10): some days improve (2022-10-26, 2022-10-27, 2022-11-05) but the winter tail more than offsets them.
- The fleet loss is not uniform across ESS: ESS14 and ESS27 carry the large negative deltas; ESS17 remains positive on the key days. The mean is not a typical-day result.
